# Domain Language

## Framework

A reusable model with named constructs, meaningful relationships, decision or operating logic, evidence, and boundaries. A named sequence, activity, diagram, pattern, tool, template, checklist, or principle is not automatically a framework.

## Source

A book, paper, course, report, or proprietary model registered with its provenance before anything is built on it. A source is evidence. It does not become company property by being cited.

## Lens

A registered source that has earned a reusable model and a recurring use case, resolvable by `baseon` and applied to a real question without becoming a verdict.

## Case Fact, Source Claim, Inference, Action

The four layers every knowledge application keeps visibly separate. A case fact is verified observation. A source claim is what the model says, with its locator. An inference is how the lens may explain this case, carrying confidence and a competing explanation. An action is a reversible experiment with a success and a revision rule.
