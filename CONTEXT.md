# Domain Language

## Framework

A reusable model with named constructs, meaningful relationships, decision or operating logic, evidence, and boundaries. A named sequence, activity, diagram, pattern, tool, template, checklist, or principle is not automatically a framework.

## House Framework

A framework owned by SolutionsIMPACT. It is the primary model used in company work. External theories and research may support or challenge it but do not silently become company property.

## Supporting Research

Cited research, books, external frameworks, consulting reports, and case studies used to derive, test, or limit a house framework. Supporting Research is evidence, not the primary company framework.

## Transformation Journey

The macro architecture that carries an organization or cohort from a diagnosed starting point through several interventions, real application, evidence, governance, and an organizational or business result.

## Learning Experience

One bounded course, workshop, session, learning day, or intervention. Its owner is responsible for learner movement, real-work output, application evidence, facilitation, and delivery readiness.

## Offer Pack

A source-controlled configuration for one SolutionsIMPACT offer or version. It may define a specific phase model, promise, sizing, evidence, and handoffs without becoming universal company law.

## Produced

Evidence that a credible real-work output was created.

## Applied

Evidence that the output or changed behavior was used in real work.

## Impact Proven

Evidence that applied work contributed to a team, organization, or business result.

## Breadcrumb

An intermediate signal such as satisfaction, confidence, room energy, or presentation quality. It helps improve the experience but does not prove impact.
