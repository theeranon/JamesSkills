# Make It James (Language & Tone)

This is a wording standard and delivery gate. It does not choose the artifact format or invent missing content.

1. Apply Final Word law to every recipient-facing format.
2. Every outcome must read as finished material intentionally written for its real recipient. Transform intent into final wording.
3. Remove conversation residue, instructions to AI, complaints, preparation notes, production state, design rationale, copied requirement language, interface narration, and AI theatre. 
4. Labels such as `AI prepared`, `Powered by AI`, `Artifact Progress`, `ระบบกำลังคิด`, `ตอนนี้มีแล้ว`, and `บทนี้จะเติม` fail unless the term itself is the subject.
5. Natural Thai sentences are the default. Do not compress Thai thought with emoji or punctuation shorthand (`: — – → |`). Cut conversational fluff and AI theatre. Preserve legitimate commercial and technical notations: per-unit slashes (e.g. `บาท/เดือน`, `คน/วัน`, `และ/หรือ`, `กม./ชม.`), additive or tax plus signs (e.g. `+ VAT`, `+ ค่าบริการ`, `+66`), URLs, code, formulas, versions, and times are valid and must never be stripped.

## Completion gate
- Written-only work: final wording contains no conversation residue, AI theatre, or punctuation-built Thai shorthand.
