# Research And Provenance

Research is required. Unsupported invention is prohibited.

## Source roles

- Primary research and original theory sources establish constructs and mechanisms.
- Official framework owners and books establish the author's model and terminology.
- Peer-reviewed studies test claims, limits, and competing explanations.
- Global consulting white papers provide current practice patterns and benchmarks, not universal scientific proof.
- International case studies and teaching cases provide context-specific evidence, not automatic causality.
- Reviewed internal cases establish SolutionsIMPACT practice, constraints, and observed outcomes.
- James's direct decisions establish company ownership, naming, and operating rules; they do not manufacture external evidence.

## Source ledger

Record for every material source:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Source ID | Stable local identifier |
| Identity | Author or organization, title, edition or date |
| Type | Research, official framework, book, white paper, case, or internal evidence |
| Access | URL, DOI, ISBN, or approved internal locator |
| Claim used | Precise paraphrase of what the source supports |
| Limitation | What it does not establish |
| Rights | Public, licensed, reviewed-private, or restricted |
| Retrieved | Date when freshness matters |

Use current web research when the source, claim, market practice, or evidence may have changed. Prefer primary sources for technical or scientific claims. Independent evidence must challenge important commercial or causal claims.

## Synthesis boundary

SolutionsIMPACT may synthesize an original framework from cited evidence and verified internal cases. It may not:

- copy a protected diagram, scoring system, assessment, or substantial text
- rename another owner's framework and claim ownership
- hide a direct adaptation that needs attribution or permission
- convert one anecdote or one course design into company-wide law
- present correlation, satisfaction, or presentation quality as organizational impact

Public material may show the approved name, visual, promise, and permitted use cases. Restricted research notes and internal decision logic remain private unless the owner explicitly publishes them.
