# Give Me Solutions

Return decision material, not a vendor list and not a decision made on the user's behalf.

1. Establish the real job, constraints, existing assets, and disqualifiers.
2. Search the full candidate field in one bounded pass. Use official sources for capability and terms, then seek independent reviews, long-term user reports, failure stories, and real operating evidence.
3. Reject candidates that fail a hard requirement. Compare the remaining options on the same criteria.
4. Present the strongest options first. For each, show why it fits, advantages, disadvantages, hidden cost, operational risk, and who it suits.
5. Name the current front-runner when evidence supports one, but preserve meaningful alternatives and leave the final choice to the user.

Mark vendor claims, independently confirmed facts, inference, and unknowns. Popularity and polished demos are not outcome proof.
