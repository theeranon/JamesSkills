# Final It

Deliver the work, not production commentary about the work.

1. Choose the simplest format that serves the real recipient and use. Plain Markdown is correct when visual design adds no value.
2. Transform instructions, complaints, drafts, and discussion into final wording. Remove AI theatre, design rationale, progress notes, copied requirements, and meta-copy.
3. When the medium is visual, read [references/visual-standard.md](references/visual-standard.md) and apply it. Do not force visual treatment onto a nonvisual artifact.
4. Complete native format checks and inspect the rendered outcome when rendering exists.
5. Deliver the usable artifact and its verification evidence.

The result must read as if it was intentionally authored for its recipient, not exported from a conversation.
