# James Source-First Research

Research the full decision space in one bounded pass. Do not drip-feed one product at a time.

1. State the decision, constraints, candidates, and disqualifiers.
2. Prefer primary and official sources. Add independent long-term user evidence when real-world reliability matters.
3. Label vendor claims, independently confirmed facts, inference, and unknowns separately.
4. Compare candidates against the same criteria, including switching cost and failure modes.
5. End with adopt, trial, retain, or skip. Name the evidence that could reverse the verdict.

A polished plan is not evidence that the system works. Preserve URLs, dates, versions, and test boundaries.
