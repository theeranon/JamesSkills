# AI Transformation Journey Five-Phase Reference

Status: verified for the SolutionsIMPACT AI Transformation Journey offer from James's direct Lark message dated 2026-08-12 and its later published derivative. This does not prove a universal Learning Experience Transformation Journey model.

The internal phase IDs below preserve that offer's operational pattern. Marketing labels are display choices and must not become the logic source.

## 1. Phase Contract

| Internal phase | Common display label | Responsibility | Typical evidence and handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pre` | Diagnose | Diagnose the organization, cohort, current capability, business pressure, readiness, and target result before the first workshop. | Confirmed scope, baseline, gap map, participant segmentation, success measures, and workshop design decisions. |
| `workshop` | Workshop | Build capability through several bounded learning days while participants create real work, use cases, or projects. Adapt from evidence instead of delivering a fixed content list. | Daily learner evidence, usable artifacts, project backlog, progress state, and coaching briefs. |
| `coaching_consult` | Coaching | Use mentor, coach, or consultation sessions to unblock, test, improve, and carry work toward implementation. | Updated project evidence, risks, decisions, owner actions, and readiness for showcase. |
| `showcase` | Showcase | Present and compare results, facilitate exchange across teams or business units, and give leaders one decision surface. | Demonstrations, pitches, cross-unit learning, selection criteria, and leadership decisions. |
| `after_transformation` | Transform | Consolidate the journey into a Transformation Report, reusable organizational assets, and a next-stage strategy decision. | Baseline comparison, value and behavior evidence, lessons, asset ownership, scale or stop decision, and next-step consultation. |

For the SolutionsIMPACT AI Transformation offer, the working language that preceded the display labels was:

```text
Pre
Workshop
Coaching Consult
Pitching or Present or Meetup
After Transformation
```

Within that offer, do not replace this spine with an older Phase 0 to Phase 4 sample or a generic learning model. Outside that offer, do not import this spine without an approved source.

## 2. Phase Design Fields

Every phase must answer:

- why this phase exists
- what enters from the prior phase
- who participates and who decides
- what intervention happens
- what tangible output is produced
- what evidence is captured
- what changes in the next phase because of that evidence
- who owns the handoff and by when
- what fallback applies if the exit condition is not met

A phase name without these fields is a decorative timeline.

## 3. SolutionsIMPACT Sizing Pattern

Use this only when SolutionsIMPACT program sizing is in scope:

- S: one department or team, normally 2 to 3 workshop days
- M: several teams inside one business unit, normally 5 workshop days
- L: several business units, normally 6 to 8 workshop days
- XL: enterprise rollout with parallel cohorts, normally 6 to 8 workshop days per cohort

Every size retains all five phase responsibilities. Scope and workshop intensity vary. Do not silently remove diagnose, coaching, showcase, or final transformation work to make a smaller package look cheaper.

Coaching commonly uses 3 to 5 sessions and the final phase commonly includes a Transformation Report and one next-step consultation. Treat counts as defaults to confirm, not immutable facts for every client.

## 4. Composition With Course Design

Composition runs one way:

```text
organization outcome and sponsor
-> five-phase macro map
-> phase outputs and handoffs
-> workshop or intervention briefs
-> design-the-course for each bounded learning experience
-> evidence returns to the macro journey
```

`design-the-course` owns learner movement, modules, practice, facilitation, and delivery readiness inside one bounded experience. It does not change sponsor outcomes, phase order, governance, portfolio measures, or the final organization-level promise.

The journey owner reviews child designs for duplication, gaps, progression, consistent language, cross-day through-line, and evidence continuity.

## 5. Measurement Trace

Define the evidence chain before promising transformation:

```text
baseline
-> learning and practice evidence
-> project or work evidence
-> implementation evidence
-> showcase decision
-> final business and capability comparison
-> next-stage decision
```

Participation, satisfaction, and presentation quality may support the record but do not by themselves prove transformation.

Maintain a promise traceability ledger:

| Promised requirement | Authority | Phase | Output and evidence | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| accepted scope or deliverable | source or decision | one or more internal phase IDs | observable proof | accountable owner | confirmed, provisional, deferred, rejected, or complete |

Every accepted promise must reach a phase and evidence owner. Conflicting, low-value, or infeasible requests must receive an explicit decision rather than vanishing or being forced into the agenda.

For AI programs, real outputs may include improved work practices, delegated agent workflows, automation or internal systems, measurable use cases, reusable playbooks, and a maintained Company Brain. Adapt the asset type to the actual transformation rather than forcing AI examples into every program.
