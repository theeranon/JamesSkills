# James Visual and Final Word Standard

## Visual defaults

- Use IBM Plex Sans Thai for Thai, English, and numbers. Do not introduce a separate Latin font.
- Compact, calm, information-dense composition. Body line-height 1.35 to 1.45; dense metadata 1.2 to 1.3.
- Control height 32px to 38px, panel padding 12px to 16px, section gap 12px to 20px unless content or accessibility requires more.
- Rectangular surfaces use 6px radius. Truly circular objects remain circular.
- Prefer typography, alignment, grouping, rows, tables, and neutral surfaces over decoration.

## Banned pattern

Never attach a thick or colored vertical stripe to the left edge of a card, panel, alert, list item, or section. This includes `border-left`, positioned bars, and pseudo-elements used as decoration or status rails. A thin neutral structural divider, quotation rule, or real timeline is allowed.

## Cards, pills, chips, and color

- Use a card only for an independent state, interaction, reorderable unit, or to prevent a real reading error.
- Use pills and chips only for interactive filters, removable selections, or compact status scanned across many rows.
- Dates, owners, categories, metadata, and explanation remain plain text.
- One primary accent per page. Red, amber, and green carry semantic status only.
- No decorative gradients, rainbow sections, or ornamental status color.

## Final Word

Every outcome must read as finished material for its recipient. Remove complaints, instructions to AI, preparation notes, design rationale, progress narration, copied requirement language, and AI theatre.

Use natural Thai sentences. Avoid emoji and decorative punctuation in Thai prose. Exact URLs, code, formulas, paths, versions, times, ratios, and direct quotations are exceptions.

## Completion

Verify the rendered font rather than trusting a CSS family declaration. Inspect desktop and mobile. Fail generic AI-dashboard appearance, clipped Thai marks, unnecessary scrolling, ornamental rails, excessive cards, excessive pills, and meta-copy.
