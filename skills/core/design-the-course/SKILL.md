# Design The Course

Pilot only. Do not present the package name or framework map as approved.

Use James's Learning Experience Design discipline, abbreviated LED. The newest direct correction places Transformative Productivity System inside LED. Older source material describes the relationship differently, so preserve that ontology conflict until James locks the canonical model. Own one bounded learning experience or one day or intervention inside a larger program. The outcome is a credible change in how the learner sees, decides, or acts. An artifact is evidence of that change, not the outcome by itself.

Read [references/method.md](references/method.md) before designing or auditing a course.

## Boundary

- Stay at course, workshop, session, or learning-day level.
- Route organization transformation spanning sponsors, governance, and several interventions to the macro-journey workflow. Do not assume a five-phase spine unless the approved offer or source requires it.
- Duration alone does not decide the route. Decide from who owns the final outcome.
- Keep the narrower knowledge-transfer course as an optional pattern. It is not the definition of LED.

## Workflow

1. Resolve authority and build the Reality Snapshot. Separate confirmed facts, working assumptions, and exact gaps.
2. Derive in this order: real learner or business tension, person being transformed, new way of seeing or deciding or acting, credible evidence, then artifact, lesson, activity, and interface.
3. Lock one learner movement from A to B, the barrier, course promise, boundary, success evidence, and one continuous case where useful.
4. Map the experience across Before, During, and After. Every unit must complete one causal chain: `before state -> trigger -> activity or practice -> output -> after state`.
5. Build each teaching move as `question or misconception -> principle -> example or evidence -> reasoning -> decision or action`.
6. Add adaptive facilitation: learner state, teaching intent, facilitator move, expected signal, and fallback for each meaningful time block.
7. Check trainer, TA, assets, timing, delivery risk, and economics. Slides do not make a course ready.
8. Produce detailed media only after the content spine is coherent. After delivery, capture evidence and update the reusable rules.

If source information is incomplete, finish every source-independent part, label the draft `provisional`, and surface only the missing decision that changes the design. Never fill a client promise or company framework from generic theory.

## Required Design Pack

- authority and gap record
- request coverage ledger showing every requested topic or requirement as keep, transform, defer, or reject with a causal reason and destination
- Reality Snapshot
- course thesis, promise, boundary, learner A and B, and success evidence
- Before, During, and After journey map
- module or time map with intent, activity, output, evidence, and fallback
- facilitation map
- learner artifact and definition of done
- trainer, TA, asset, and delivery-readiness check
- decision log and post-delivery debrief

Adapt the final format to the request. Do not create all files mechanically when one coherent document can carry the complete design.

## Ready Gate

Call the design ready only when:

- every element has a causal reason for moving the same learner from A to B
- the time budget fits and every activity earns its place
- practice resembles the real decision or action and produces observable evidence
- the running case, outputs, and transitions remain coherent
- trainer and support roles can explain, adapt, and recover the experience
- assets are authoritative and usable at the moment of need
- every requested item has an explicit disposition; nothing is silently dropped or retained by habit
- no unsupported framework, promise, fact, or completion claim remains
