# Learning Experience Design

Own one bounded learning experience. A multi-day course can still be LED when it owns one learner or capability movement. An organization transformation spanning several interventions, sponsors, governance, and business impact belongs to `transformation-journey`.

Read [references/method.md](references/method.md) before designing or auditing. Use [assets/learning-experience-design-pack.html](assets/learning-experience-design-pack.html) for the final report.

## Framework use

Learning Experience Design is the operating discipline, not one fixed framework. Search the SolutionsIMPACT house library and select the smallest house design authority whose kind, lifecycle, evidence, and permitted scope fit the requested readiness. A framework, method, principle, and pattern are different objects; never label a principle or pattern as a framework. Transformative Productivity System is one available house framework inside LED, not the definition of LED and not a transformation-journey timeline.

External theories and research may support the design, but the primary design authority must be cited and house-owned. An approved house framework, principle, pattern, or method may be used within its registered scope. A candidate or source-gap asset may only inform a clearly labeled provisional, reversible skeleton; it cannot satisfy the delivery-ready gate. If the design needs reusable framework-level decision logic and no approved house framework fits, use `build-framework`; do not invent one inside the course design.

## Workflow

1. Resolve source authority, learner and business reality, constraints, and exact gaps.
2. Infer the internal operation from the request: create, audit, repair, or improve. Do not ask the user to choose this label.
3. Work backward from the organizational or work impact to applied behavior, evidence, learner output, and experience design.
4. Lock the learner movement from A to B, barrier, promise, boundary, and evidence chain before choosing modules or activities.
5. Select and disclose each house design authority with its kind, lifecycle, supporting research, applicability, and limitations. Never upgrade a principle or pattern into a framework through wording.
6. Design Before, During, and After. Every block needs intent, learner action, output, evidence, transition, and fallback.
7. Verify trainer, facilitator, TA, assets, timing, delivery risk, economics, and transfer into real work.
8. Account for every requested item as keep, transform, defer, or reject with a causal reason.
9. Produce one executive HTML Design Pack. Call it delivery-ready only when every source, authority, facilitation, asset, timing, and evidence gate passes; otherwise label it provisional and name the exact remaining gates. Create additional media only when the request requires them.
10. After delivery, capture application and impact evidence. Feed a reusable improvement to `build-framework` as a candidate; never rewrite an approved framework silently.

## Success

Use three evidence states:

- Produced: the learner created a credible real-work output.
- Applied: the output or behavior changed real work.
- Impact Proven: evidence shows contribution to a team, organization, or business result.

Satisfaction is a breadcrumb for improving the experience. It is never the success definition.

## Ready gate

The design is ready only when every element helps the same learner move from A to B, practice resembles real work, evidence is observable, time fits, delivery roles can adapt and recover, requested items are accounted for, and no unsupported framework, promise, fact, or impact claim remains.
