# Satir Model

Independent private synthesis. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by the Virginia Satir Global Network, the Satir Institute of the Pacific, or any Satir body. `VIRGINIA SATIR` is a registered US service mark held by the Virginia Satir Global Network for training services; the concept names used here are not claimed as marks.

## Use for

- Work beneath a stuck behaviour to the feeling, assumption, expectation, and want that hold it in place.
- Read which communication stance a person falls into under stress, and choose where to make contact.
- Run a coaching conversation that ends in the person's own committed, rehearsed next action.

## Do not use for

- Personality typing. The model's own literature states the stances are ways of surviving stress, not personality categories.
- Anything clinical: diagnosis, trauma processing, or work with a person whose daily functioning is impaired.
- Any claim that the model is evidence-based, validated, or proven. It is not, in any setting.
- Organisational or performance outcomes. No published outcome study exists for the business applications.

## Core move

Enter at the visible behaviour the person already said they want to change, ask permission to go beneath it, descend one layer at a time through questions only, then come back up to a concrete action and rehearse it. The value is in the descent being the person's own, not in the vocabulary.

## Naming

The umbrella term is **Satir Model** or **Satir Growth Model**. `STST`, Satir Transformational Systemic Therapy, is the name of the clinical application only and must not be used for coaching work. **Satya** is a misspelling: it is an unrelated Sanskrit word meaning truth, the second of Patanjali's yamas. The correct romanisation is **Satir**.
