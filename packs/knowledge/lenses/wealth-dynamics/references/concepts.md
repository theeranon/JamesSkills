# Wealth Dynamics / Talent Dynamics Concepts

## WD-001 — One Dynamics model, two application contexts

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-dynamics-official-eguide#four-energies-eight-profiles`; `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p21-p38`; `framework-evidence-audit-2026#framework-map`
- Confidence: `supported`

Wealth Dynamics applies the four-energy and eight-profile model to entrepreneurial value creation and leverage. Talent Dynamics adapts the same topology to roles, leadership, communication, trust, and team flow. The application context changes; the underlying profile model does not become a separate science.

## WD-002 — Value and leverage are the entrepreneurial application

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-dynamics-official-eguide#wealth-equation`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The Wealth Dynamics application asks how a person most naturally creates value and builds leverage. Use that language to generate work-design hypotheses, not to predict wealth or competence.

## TD-001 — Flow and trust are the team application's intended outcomes

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p3-p5`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The guide presents Talent Dynamics as the team and organizational application of the shared Dynamics model, with emphasis on natural work patterns, leadership, team contribution, trust, and flow. These are framework claims, not independently proven outcomes.

## TD-002 — A profile is described as a path, not a box

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p3-p5`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The source itself cautions against treating a profile as a fixed container. The safe use is to test work conditions and role patterns rather than declare an immutable personality.

## TD-003 — Four base frequencies and a fifth purpose dimension

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p17-p18`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The model uses Dynamo for initiation and innovation, Blaze for people and communication, Tempo for timing and service, and Steel for systems, data, and completion. It also describes Spirit as a purpose or contribution dimension. These are the model's own constructs.

## TD-004 — Eight-profile topology

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p21-p38`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The guide places Creator at Dynamo, Star between Dynamo and Blaze, Supporter at Blaze, Deal Maker between Blaze and Tempo, Trader at Tempo, Accumulator between Tempo and Steel, Lord at Steel, and Mechanic between Steel and Dynamo.

## TD-005 — Profile roles are hypotheses about contribution

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p21-p38`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

In compressed form, the guide associates the eight profiles with initiating, promoting, leading people, nurturing deals, negotiating in context, reliable project delivery, analysis and measurement, and improving or completing systems. Do not convert these associations into exclusive job eligibility.

## TD-006 — Trust is split into five proposed contribution areas

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p17-p18`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The guide maps trust to innovation, communication, service, measurement, and spirit. Operationally, this can prompt a team to ask where a person is consistently relied upon and where support is required.

## TD-007 — Leadership style varies by frequency in the model

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015#pdf-p39-p40`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The guide characterizes Dynamo leadership as task and creation oriented, Blaze as people oriented, Tempo as activity and context oriented, Steel as data oriented, and Spirit as purpose oriented. Treat this as a discussion map, not a universal leadership law.

## TD-008 — Classification strength is publicly unverified

- Evidence: `[independent evidence]`
- Source: `framework-evidence-audit-2026#evidence-audit`
- Confidence: `supported`

The targeted audit did not identify a public independent validation study or technical manual publishing norms, reliability, test-retest stability, factor structure, predictive validity, language invariance, or adverse-impact data for the exact instrument. Therefore an inferred profile remains a working hypothesis.
