# Wealth Spectrum

Independent private synthesis. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a substitute for an official Genius Group or Wealth Spectrum assessment or accredited practitioner.

## Use for

- Locate the current operating constraint in a wealth or enterprise journey.
- Distinguish personal skill and cash generation from a team, system, or capital base that works beyond the owner.
- Ask what capability must become reliable before pursuing the next stage.

## Do not use for

- Treat the generic full pack as anyone's personal assessment; it covers every level and profile and is never anyone's personal assessment.
- Assign a level from lifestyle, confidence, revenue alone, or a generic report pack.
- Treat a dated level hypothesis as a permanent identity.
- Replace cash-flow, liquidity, debt, margin, owner-dependence, and execution evidence.

## Core move

Use the level vocabulary only after checking current facts. The useful output is the present constraint and next capability, not a flattering color label.
