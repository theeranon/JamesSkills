# Wealth Spectrum Limitations

## Evidence boundary

- Targeted public research found no independently accessible technical manual or validation study for this exact framework as of 2026-08-28. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no private evidence exists.
- The source is first-party framework material. Its nine-stage structure is an author model, not verified financial law.
- The full pack covers every level and profile. A generic pack is never anyone's personal assessment and cannot establish that a given person sits at any particular level.
- A level inferred from business facts remains a dated working hypothesis. Cash flow, liquidity, debt, market conditions, and owner dependence can change it.
- Differences between older wealth-creation language and newer financial-literacy wording must remain versioned rather than silently merged.

## Prohibited uses

- Do not present an inferred level as an official assessment result.
- Do not use a level as the sole basis for credit, investment, compensation, hiring, or clinical decisions.
- Do not make wealth, revenue, or success predictions from a label.
- Do not reproduce official reports, diagrams, assessment items, scoring, or branded training material.

## Fit and consent

Wealth Spectrum is not automatically relevant to every person or question. Use it only when the subject and purpose make financial or enterprise-stage reflection useful. For another person, prefer consent and observable evidence over unsolicited ranking.

## Revision rule

Attach `as_of`, confidence, contradictions, and review triggers to every reusable stage hypothesis. Re-run after a material cash-flow, debt, business-model, ownership, or team-capacity change.
