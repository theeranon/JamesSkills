# Wealth Spectrum Concepts

## WS-001 — A staged capacity lens

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p3-p5`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The source presents nine stages across three broad prisms. It uses them to discuss the kind of value, cash flow, assets, trust, and operating capacity a person or enterprise can currently manage.

## WS-002 — Foundation prism

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p3-p5`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

Infrared, Red, and Orange describe movement from worsening debt, through bare survival, to income that still depends heavily on continued personal work. Treat these as model vocabulary, not moral judgments about a person.

## WS-003 — Enterprise prism

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p3-p5`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

Yellow, Green, and Blue describe a shift from monetized personal mastery, to a profitable team and enterprise, to broader capital and investment orchestration. The central operating question changes from personal production to repeatable organizational capacity.

## WS-004 — Alchemy prism

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p3-p5`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

Indigo, Violet, and Ultraviolet describe progressively wider trust, market-shaping, and legacy influence. The source's claims at these levels are conceptual and should not be treated as independently verified financial science.

## WS-005 — Level is a current constraint hypothesis

The safest operational use is to ask which constraint is visible now and what evidence would move the hypothesis. Do not convert the color into personality, worth, or a permanent rank.

## WS-006 — Yellow describes valuable but owner-dependent mastery

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p71-p77`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The Yellow material associates progress with positive cash flow from market mastery and independence, while warning that the flow still relies on the central player. The bottleneck question is whether value and decisions remain dependent on one person.

## WS-007 — Yellow-to-Green is an enterprise-capability transition

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p71-p77`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The source frames the next transition around a stronger enterprise identity, recurring operating rhythm, shared metrics, deliberate team synchronization, and less owner-centered control. It distinguishes delegation with feedback from abandoning responsibility.

## WS-008 — Profile-specific advice is conditional

- Evidence: `[official claim]`
- Source: `wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011#pdf-p78-p79`
- Confidence: `source-faithful`

The source overlays the eight Wealth Dynamics profiles onto stage transitions. For example, its Lord-at-Yellow paragraph emphasizes back-end detail and efficiency while warning about becoming both linchpin and bottleneck. This is usable only when the profile input is separately established; the generic full pack proves no individual's profile.
