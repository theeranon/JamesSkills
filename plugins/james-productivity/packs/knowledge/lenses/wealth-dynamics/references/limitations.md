# Wealth Dynamics / Talent Dynamics Limitations

## Evidence boundary

- A stored profile label is not an official result. Any profile for any subject remains inferred, and is downgraded to a working hypothesis with its date and alternatives, unless that person supplies or confirms an official report.
- Talent Dynamics and Wealth Dynamics share one underlying model, but their team and entrepreneurial applications must still be named accurately. Wealth Spectrum is a different stage model and must not be merged into this lens.
- Targeted public research found no independently accessible technical manual or validation study for this exact instrument as of 2026-08-28. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no private evidence exists.
- The guide's profile, trust, flow, role, and performance associations are first-party claims. Usage volume and similarity to other personality systems do not validate the scoring or outcomes.
- The guide includes time-sensitive public-figure examples. Those examples are omitted because they age badly and do not prove a profile.
- Language and cultural equivalence of translated assessments were not independently established in the reviewed public materials.

## Prohibited uses

- Do not build or present a homemade quiz as the official Talent Dynamics assessment.
- Do not copy assessment items, response options, scoring rules, reports, diagrams, icons, or training material.
- Do not label an unconsenting person from chat or observation alone.
- Do not use the profile as the sole or decisive basis for hiring, firing, pay, promotion, credit, investment, or clinical decisions.
- Do not promise trust, productivity, revenue, or flow from a profile intervention.

## Rights boundary

The audited corporate filing describes product and practitioner licensing paths. Private storage does not make copied proprietary expression safe. Use original paraphrase, attribution, and independent experiments; obtain appropriate permission before branded training, commercial adaptation, or official debrief work.

## Revision rule

Store official results only as user-declared inputs with date and version. Store inferred results as working hypotheses with confidence, alternatives, and disconfirming evidence. Revise after role, environment, team, health, or workload changes.
