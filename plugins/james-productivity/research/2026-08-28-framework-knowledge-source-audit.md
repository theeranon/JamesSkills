# Framework Knowledge Source Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Scope:** Wealth Dynamics, Talent Dynamics, Wealth Spectrum, and a reusable source model for future frameworks/books
**Status:** Research finding, not legal advice and not psychometric certification

## Decision

These frameworks are usable inside a private James skill **as coaching lenses and hypothesis generators**. They are not strong enough, on the public evidence found, to become a diagnostic engine, an official assessment clone, or a high-stakes decision rule.

Build the skill around a vendor-neutral knowledge contract:

1. Accept an official result only as a user-declared input.
2. Without a result, form a clearly labelled working hypothesis with alternatives and confidence.
3. Keep framework interpretation separate from verified business, financial, and behavioral evidence.
4. Turn an interpretation into small reversible experiments, then learn from observed outcomes.
5. Store citations and paraphrased claims, not copied books, assessment items, score keys, diagrams, or official report language.

This preserves the useful language of the models without pretending that the skill is Genius Group, an accredited practitioner, or an independently validated psychometric instrument.

## Research method and evidence boundary

Priority order used:

1. Current company filing and terms.
2. First-party framework guides and official product pages.
3. Publisher metadata for the first-party book.
4. Independent testing standards and peer-reviewed research relevant to the models' claims.

Targeted searches used the exact framework names with terms including `validity`, `reliability`, `psychometric`, `factor analysis`, and `test-retest`. As of 2026-08-28, I did **not identify a publicly accessible independent validation study or technical manual specifically for Wealth Dynamics, Talent Dynamics, or the Wealth Spectrum stage assignment**. This is a bounded search finding, not proof that no unpublished, private, or differently indexed study exists.

## 1. What the official models are

### Framework map

| Framework | Official purpose | Core structure | Safe interpretation inside a skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wealth Dynamics | Entrepreneur-focused profile intended to guide value creation, leverage, team, and strategy | Four base energies arranged around eight profiles | A vocabulary for preferred ways of creating and multiplying value |
| Talent Dynamics | Corporate/team adaptation of Wealth Dynamics | Same four-energy/eight-profile topology, applied to role, leadership, communication, trust, and team flow | A hypothesis about role fit and collaboration experiments |
| Wealth Spectrum | Financial/enterprise progression model | Nine levels across three broad bands | A prompt for locating the current constraint and the next capability to build |

Genius Group's 2026 filing describes Wealth Dynamics as a profiling tool created in 2002, Talent Dynamics as its corporate/team adaptation, and Wealth Spectrum as nine financial-literacy levels with level-specific challenges, solutions, and next steps. The filing also describes paid licensing paths for consultants and partners who use or adapt these products. [Genius Group, 2026 Form 20-F](https://ir.geniusgroup.net/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001493152-26-009371/0001493152-26-009371.pdf)

### Wealth Dynamics

The first-party e-guide presents four base energies:

- **Dynamo:** innovation and intuitive thinking.
- **Blaze:** people, communication, and magnifying through relationships.
- **Tempo:** timing, service, and sensory awareness of context.
- **Steel:** systems, data, detail, and multiplying through infrastructure.

It arranges eight profiles around those energies:

| Profile | Position in the official topology |
|---|---|
| Creator | Dynamo-dominant |
| Star | Dynamo + Blaze |
| Supporter | Blaze-dominant |
| Deal Maker | Blaze + Tempo |
| Trader | Tempo-dominant |
| Accumulator | Tempo + Steel |
| Lord | Steel-dominant |
| Mechanic | Steel + Dynamo |

The guide also describes two broad tensions: value through innovation versus timing, and leverage through people versus data/systems. It explicitly frames a profile as a path rather than a permanent box. [Official Wealth Dynamics e-guide](https://assessments.geniusu.com/pdf/wealth-dynamics-eguide.pdf) The official site condenses the commercial logic into `wealth = value x leverage`. [Wealth Dynamics, “The Wealth Equation”](https://www.wealthdynamics.com/the-wealth-equation)

**Skill consequence:** retain the topology and plain-language distinctions, but do not treat a profile label as a fixed identity, capability ceiling, or causal explanation of success.

### Talent Dynamics

Talent Dynamics carries the same four-energy/eight-profile map into teams. Its first-party leaders' guide applies the model to strengths, communication, productivity, role fit, leadership, trust, and flow. It claims the assessment can identify personality, strengths, values, behavior, and productive team role, but the accessible guide does not publish the measurement details needed to evaluate those claims independently. [The Leader's Guide to Talent Dynamics](https://s3.amazonaws.com/team-dynamics/The-Leaders-Guide-to-TD.pdf) Current first-party marketing continues to position it as a team and leadership system. [Talent Dynamics](https://talentdynamics.geniusu.com/)

**Skill consequence:** use it to generate questions such as “Which role conditions make this person consistently effective?” and “What communication experiment should the team try?” Do not declare innate competence, allocate employment rights, or automate hiring and promotion from a profile.

### Wealth Spectrum

The first-party Wealth Spectrum e-guide distinguishes **who/how** from **where**: Wealth Dynamics describes a preferred path; Wealth Spectrum describes the current stage. Its nine levels are:

| Band | Levels |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Infrared Victim, Red Survivor, Orange Worker |
| Enterprise | Yellow Player, Green Performer, Blue Conductor |
| Alchemy | Indigo Trustee, Violet Composer, Ultraviolet Legend |

[Official Wealth Spectrum e-guide](https://genius-u-attachments.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/file_vault/file_content/286/Wealth_Spectrum_E-Guide.pdf)

The older guide speaks in terms of wealth-creation stage, while the 2026 filing calls the nine stages financial-literacy levels. Those wordings should be versioned rather than silently merged into one scientific construct. Roger James Hamilton's 2014 book presents a related nine-step, color-coded path. [Hachette Book Group, *The Millionaire Master Plan*](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/roger-james-hamilton/the-millionaire-master-plan/9781455549238/)

**Skill consequence:** derive the stage only as a provisional lens unless James supplies an official result. Anchor advice in actual cash flow, liquidity, debt, recurring revenue, owner dependence, team capacity, and execution evidence.

## 2. Claims versus independent evidence

### Evidence audit

| Question | Official material says | Independent/public evidence found | Verdict for skill use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is the framework useful as a shared language? | Yes; guides present it as a map for entrepreneurs, leaders, and teams | A shared vocabulary can structure reflection, but utility is not the same as validated measurement | Allowed as a coaching lens |
| Does the assessment reliably assign profiles? | Official material markets an assessment and profile report | No public, independent framework-specific technical manual or validation study identified in the targeted search | Do not claim known reliability or classification stability |
| Does a profile predict performance, wealth, trust, or flow? | Marketing materials associate the systems with these outcomes | No independently replicated criterion/predictive evidence for these instruments identified | Do not promise outcomes or use labels as causal explanations |
| Does a Wealth Spectrum test objectively locate financial stage? | The [first-party test page](https://institute.geniusu.com/spectrum/) says it identifies one of nine levels and gives next steps | The targeted search found first-party and practitioner descriptions, not independent evidence for scoring accuracy, stage stability, or outcome prediction | Treat a stage as a planning hypothesis and verify it against financial facts |
| Are the categories natural psychological types? | Official topology uses named profiles | Research on a nearby Jungian type instrument found its scales behaved as continuous dimensions rather than true dichotomous types; that is caution by analogy, not a direct test of Wealth Dynamics | Treat labels as summaries of tendencies, not biological or fixed types |
| Can it drive hiring or promotion? | Talent Dynamics is sold for organizational use | Professional standards require validity for the intended use, population, fairness, and consequences | Never make it the sole or decisive employment rule |

The official GeniusU psychometrics page invokes Jung, MBTI, DISC, StrengthsFinder, and the Big Five, and calls the Big Five a benchmark. It does not provide Wealth Dynamics or Talent Dynamics reliability coefficients, norms, factor structure, test-retest results, predictive validity, language invariance, or adverse-impact analysis. [GeniusU psychometrics overview](https://app.geniusu.com/psychometrics)

An official Wealth Dynamics article itself acknowledges that personality tests are not foolproof and should not be the entire basis for critical hiring or admissions decisions. [Wealth Dynamics, “Pros and Cons of Taking a Personality Test for Entrepreneurs”](https://wealthdynamics.geniusu.com/blog/pros-cons-taking-personality-test-entrepreneurs/)

The public evidence needed for a strong psychometric claim would include:

1. Instrument and item-development method.
2. Normative sample, demographics, and language/cultural coverage.
3. Internal consistency and test-retest reliability.
4. Factor structure, scoring thresholds, classification stability, and measurement error.
5. Convergent, discriminant, criterion, predictive, fairness, and independent replication evidence.

The [APA/AERA/NCME testing standards](https://www.apa.org/science/programs/testing/standards) are the main professional reference for evaluating test validity and use. APA guidance stresses that reliability is necessary but insufficient, and that a measure must be valid and appropriate for its population and purpose. [APA Guidelines for Psychological Evaluation](https://www.apa.org/practice/guidelines/psychological-evaluations.html) The [International Test Commission Guidelines on Test Use](https://www.intestcom.org/page/15) similarly require competent, ethical, context-sensitive use.

For employment decisions, the U.S. EEOC states that a selection procedure must be job-related and properly validated for its particular use; using a vendor does not remove the employer's responsibility for adverse impact or validation. [EEOC, Employment Tests and Selection Procedures](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures)

### Relevant adjacent research, not direct validation

Wealth Dynamics' own materials associate the system with Jungian traditions and the MBTI. In a peer-reviewed study of the MBTI, McCrae and Costa found no support for true dichotomous personality types and interpreted the indices as continuous dimensions. That finding supports caution about rigid category labels, but it neither validates nor invalidates Wealth Dynamics itself. [McCrae & Costa, 1989](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-6494.1989.tb00759.x)

The separate Big Five literature includes evidence relating broad traits to job-performance criteria, but that evidence cannot be transferred to Wealth Dynamics merely because a marketing page mentions the Big Five. [Barrick & Mount, 1991](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1744-6570.1991.tb00688.x)

**Bottom line:** “related to Jung,” “inspired by old traditions,” “used by customers,” and “resembles another model” are origin or marketing narratives. None substitutes for validation of this instrument, scoring method, language version, population, or intended decision.

## 3. Intended-use boundaries

| Use | Decision | Required guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Personal reflection and coaching prompts | Use | Label as a lens; show alternatives and confidence |
| Tailoring communication or role experiments | Use | Make changes reversible; evaluate actual outcomes |
| Combining a declared profile with James's observed work history | Use | Keep observed evidence visibly separate from framework interpretation |
| Estimating a Wealth Spectrum stage | Cautious use | Call it a provisional working hypothesis; show the financial facts used |
| Generating an “official” profile without the official assessment | Do not use | Never imitate an official result or certification |
| Hiring, firing, promotion, pay, credit, investment, or clinical decisions | Do not use as a decision rule | Require independently appropriate evidence and accountable human judgment |
| Predicting wealth, team productivity, or business success | Do not claim | Present as an unverified hypothesis, never a promised outcome |
| Labeling another person who has not consented | Avoid | Discuss observed behavior and task conditions instead |

## 4. Copyright, trademark, and licensing risk

### What the first-party record shows

Genius Group's filing identifies registered **figurative** marks for Talent Dynamics and Wealth Dynamics, describes Entrepreneurs Institute/Wealth Dynamics Pte Ltd as the owner and developer of curriculum and tools, and describes paid licenses for event hosts, consultants, performance consultants, and product partners. The product-partner license specifically covers co-creating industry- or language-specific content using the tools. [Genius Group, 2026 Form 20-F](https://ir.geniusgroup.net/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001493152-26-009371/0001493152-26-009371.pdf)

The group's terms require users to respect its copyright, trademark, trade-secret, and other IP rights, and disclaim guarantees or predictions of profit. [Entrepreneurs Institute Terms](https://entrepreneursinstitute.com/Terms/)

Under U.S. copyright principles, an idea, procedure, process, system, or method is not protected by copyright as such, while the author's text, selection/arrangement, illustrations, questions, reports, and other expression can be protected. [17 U.S.C. §102(b)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf) Jurisdiction and contract rights can differ; this section is an engineering risk screen, not legal advice.

### Risk matrix for a private skill

| Proposed content/action | Risk | Safe direction |
|---|---:|---|
| Name the framework and cite it for discussion | Low to moderate | Descriptive, attributed reference; no affiliation claim |
| Paraphrase the broad topology in original language | Moderate | Cite source; add independent limits; do not reproduce expressive presentation |
| Name the eight profiles or nine levels when necessary | Moderate | Use only to identify the model; avoid branded visual mimicry |
| Use a user-supplied official result privately | Moderate | Record it as `user_declared`; do not redistribute the report |
| Put the official PDF/book in Git, even a private repo | High | Keep user-owned originals outside the repo; store metadata and citations only |
| Copy assessment questions, score keys, report paragraphs, diagrams, or training exercises | High | Do not include |
| Generate an official-looking assessment or certificate | High | Do not include; state independent/private synthesis |
| Sell an official debrief, workshop, or adaptation under the product name | High | Obtain the appropriate license and legal review first |
| Name the skill as if it were the official product | High confusion/brand risk | Use a vendor-neutral skill name |

A private repository reduces public-distribution exposure, but does not convert copied protected material into safe material and does not override assessment terms, trade-secret obligations, trademarks, or licenses.

### Safe paraphrase rule

**Generally safe design direction:**

- State the abstract framework and necessary names in concise original wording.
- Cite the exact edition/source behind each interpretation.
- Write original prompts, decision rules, examples, and experiments.
- Add caveats, counterevidence, and an explicit non-affiliation notice.
- Refer a user to the official provider when an official assessment or certified debrief matters.

**Do not store or regenerate:**

- Assessment items, response options, scoring algorithms, thresholds, or report templates.
- Extended source text, diagrams, profile artwork, color/layout systems, facilitator material, or role-model lists.
- A reconstructed test that produces the provider's official labels as though it were authorized.
- Full copyrighted books or paid reports inside the repository.

Suggested notice:

> Independent private synthesis for reflection and planning. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a substitute for an official Genius Group, Wealth Dynamics, Talent Dynamics, or Wealth Spectrum assessment or accredited practitioner.

## 5. Source architecture for this skill and future books

Do not turn `SKILL.md` into a book dump. Keep procedure and knowledge separate.

```text
sources/
  wealth-dynamics-official-eguide.json
  talent-dynamics-genius-guide-2015.json
  wealth-spectrum-fullpack-2011.json
lenses/
  wealth-dynamics/      # canonical; talent-dynamics is an alias
    manifest.json
    index.md
    references/
  wealth-spectrum/      # separate model, same creator lineage
    manifest.json
    index.md
    references/
```

Record creator lineage and model identity separately. Wealth Dynamics and Talent Dynamics share one underlying profile model. Wealth Spectrum is a different stage model and must not be merged merely because the creator is the same.

### `source.yaml`

Minimum fields:

```yaml
id: wealth-dynamics
title: Wealth Dynamics
creator: Roger James Hamilton
edition_or_version: "official e-guide, accessed 2026-08-28"
source_type: first_party_guide
canonical_url: https://assessments.geniusu.com/pdf/wealth-dynamics-eguide.pdf
rights_basis: citation_and_original_paraphrase_only
original_file_location: external_to_git
acquired_at: 2026-08-28
scope:
  - entrepreneurial preference lens
not_for_use:
  - official assessment reconstruction
  - high_stakes_decisions
```

### `claims.md`

Every knowledge unit should carry:

- A stable claim ID.
- Original paraphrase.
- Page/chapter or precise URL.
- Source version/date.
- One evidence label: `[official claim]`, `[independent evidence]`, `[James rule]`, or `[inference]`.
- Confidence and known conflicts.

Never merge an official claim and independent evidence into one unlabeled sentence.

### `applications.md`

Store James-owned transformation logic:

- Questions the framework helps ask.
- Decision patterns it may illuminate.
- Reversible experiments.
- Evidence required before escalating a conclusion.
- Ways the model interacts with James's real constraints.

This is the defensible, reusable asset. It transforms source knowledge rather than copying it.

### `limits.md`

Store:

- Claims with no public validation found.
- Counterevidence and adjacent evidence.
- High-stakes exclusions.
- Cultural/language concerns.
- Outdated or unreliable examples.
- Known differences between editions.

The Wealth Dynamics e-guide, for example, uses time-sensitive public-figure success examples. Those examples should never become evidence for a profile or remain in the durable knowledge layer without current verification.

### Ingestion gate for each new book or framework

A source enters the skill only after all five gates pass:

1. **Provenance:** exact author, edition, date, URL/ISBN, and source owner recorded.
2. **Rights:** lawful access and allowed use recorded; no full source committed to Git by default.
3. **Extraction:** claims paraphrased with page/chapter citations and evidence labels.
4. **Challenge:** limitations, counterevidence, conflicts, and outdated examples recorded.
5. **Behavior:** trigger, output, non-goals, and fixtures prove how the knowledge changes an answer.

## 6. Recommended runtime contract

### Input states

The skill must distinguish:

```yaml
framework_result:
  status: official_user_declared | working_hypothesis | unknown
  source: user_reported | imported_private_report | inferred
  version: string | unknown
  confidence: 0.0-1.0
```

Do not silently convert `inferred` into `official_user_declared`.

### Reasoning order

1. **Question and desired outcome.** What decision or change is James trying to make?
2. **Verified facts.** Cash flow, role history, behavior, commitments, results, and constraints.
3. **Declared framework input.** Official result if James has one; otherwise `unknown`.
4. **Lens.** One or more interpretations, source/version, confidence, and contrary evidence.
5. **Action.** One to three reversible experiments with observable success/failure conditions.
6. **Learning.** Capture outcome; revise the working hypothesis instead of defending the label.

### Output example

```text
Evidence
- James consistently starts new offers quickly but leaves operating handoff incomplete.

Framework lens
- Creator/Dynamo is one plausible interpretation, not an official result.
- Alternative: role overload or missing operating capacity may explain the same behavior.

Experiment
- For the next offer, James owns concept and first proof; a named operator owns the release checklist.
- Measure cycle time, rework, and tasks returned to James for 14 days.

Revision rule
- Keep the lens only if the intervention improves the measured outcome.
```

## 7. Build recommendation

Create **one vendor-neutral framework skill**, with these frameworks as source modules. Do not create a branded Wealth Dynamics/Talent Dynamics assessor.

Recommended capability boundary:

- **Does:** selects an appropriate framework lens, explains it in plain language, compares alternative interpretations, and turns it into a testable action.
- **Does not:** administer proprietary assessments, certify profiles, promise business outcomes, or make high-stakes decisions.
- **Can grow:** add books and frameworks through the same provenance/rights/claims/limits/fixtures gate.
- **Can personalize:** learn from James's observed results while retaining the source/version and never overwriting evidence with a label.

The robust abstraction is not “a library of personality labels.” It is **a cited library of decision lenses with explicit limits and feedback loops**.
